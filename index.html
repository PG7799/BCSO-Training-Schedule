Schedule a training

Anyone can submit a session for their division. Overlapping sessions are sent to Training Command for review.

Automatic conflict checkingThe system checks every scheduled training. If the requested time overlaps another session, it is sent to the Training Command approval queue instead of being scheduled automatically.

Scheduling guidelines

  • Check the department calendar before selecting a time.
  • Include a clear title and instructor.
  • Allow time for personnel to travel and prepare.
  • Contact Training Command Staff if cancellation code is forgotten or lost.
Need an exception?

Overlapping sessions are submitted for review. Authorized Training Command members can approve or deny them from the restricted Overlap Requests tab.
